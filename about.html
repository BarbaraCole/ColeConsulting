Founder & Principal

Barbara Cole

Barbara brings over 20 years of experience to the intersection of psychology, assessment, and fairness. A bilingual Talent Assessment and Development Specialist, she has shaped careers and organizations from the inside out by designing tools for staffing and development, creating competency frameworks at the core, technical, and leadership levels, and guiding professionals toward best practices that are both rigorous and humane.

Her expertise spans a wide range of applied psychology and psychometrics: from constructing different types of tests, questionnaires, and rating scales to interpreting results and delivering meaningful development feedback. Fluent in both official languages and in quantitative and qualitative research methods, she has conducted training in academic and government settings.

Known for her genuine passion for human assessment and well-being and her talent for translating insights into real-world impact, she creates learning experiences and tools that are practical, evidence-based, and engaging.

She holds a Master's degree in Psychology and a Bachelor's degree in French, and has authored peer-reviewed scholarly publications in social psychology.
