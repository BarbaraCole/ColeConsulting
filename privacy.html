Privacy Policy

Last updated August 20, 2026.

Cole Consulting & Solutions ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, how it's used, and how you can reach us about it.

Information we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, and message, along with optional fields for company name and area of interest. We do not collect any information from visitors who don't submit the form.

How we use it

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not use it for marketing lists, and we do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties.

Where it's stored

Contact form submissions are processed by Netlify Forms, the third-party form-handling service built into our website host, and forwarded to a Cole Consulting & Solutions email address that we check periodically. Your information is not stored in any other database or system.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or any third-party tracking scripts.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete any information you've submitted at any time by emailing hello@coleassessment.ca. We'll respond promptly.

Retention

We retain contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, or until you ask us to delete them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@coleassessment.ca.
