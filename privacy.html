Privacy Policy

Last updated August 20, 2026.

I respect your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, how it's used, and how you can reach me about it.

Information I collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, I collect the information you provide: your name, email address, and message, along with optional fields for company name and area of interest. I do not collect any information from visitors who don't submit the form.

How I use it

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry. I do not use it for marketing lists, and I do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties.

Where it's stored

Contact form submissions are processed by Netlify Forms, the third-party form-handling service built into this website's host, and forwarded to an email address that I check periodically. Your information is not stored in any other database or system.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or any third-party tracking scripts.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct, or delete any information you've submitted at any time by emailing hello@coleassessment.ca. I'll respond promptly.

Retention

I retain contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, or until you ask me to delete them.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
