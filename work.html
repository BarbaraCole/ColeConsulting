01 — Assessment Strategies

Planning the right
way to measure.

Assessment Architect is a browser-based planning guide that walks test developers through the design decisions that matter most — three planning dimensions (Psychometric Planning, Equity & Legal Compliance, and Strategic & Operational Viability), 30 questions, and a detailed test specification that flows into a final design-readiness report with colour-coded ratings and category scores grounded in professional testing standards. No login, no cost, and nothing proprietary is ever entered or shared. Where the Assessment Auditor diagnoses quality after the fact, the Architect builds it in from the start — it answers "how ready are we to build one?"

02 — Tools

Instruments built
for real decisions.

Two instruments — one evaluates what already exists, the other measures what's possible.

Assessment Auditor

Assessment Auditor is a free tool that helps test developers evaluate the quality of an existing talent assessment, now fully bilingual — every question, response, result, and report is available in French and English. It's mapped to professional assessment standards, covering psychometric integrity, equity and defensibility, and strategic and operational fit, while reflecting the realities of applied practice. The tool generates an automatic report with a colour-coded overall rating, category scores on a five-point scale, and a visual quality spectrum meter — a clear, standards-based read on how good a tool really is, for newcomers and seasoned test developers alike.
