Assessment & Talent Solutions

Tools for effective
talent decisions.

Innovative digital tools for building, evaluating, and developing talent — grounded in assessment science.

01 — Assessment Architect

Planning the right
way to measure talent.

Assessment Architect is a browser-based planning guide that walks test developers through the design decisions that matter most — three planning dimensions (Psychometric Planning, Equity & Legal Compliance, and Strategic & Operational Viability), 30 questions, and a detailed test specification that flows into a final design-readiness report with colour-coded ratings and category scores grounded in professional testing standards. No login, no cost, and nothing proprietary is ever entered or shared. Where the Assessment Auditor diagnoses quality after the fact, the Architect builds it in from the start — it answers "how ready are we to build one?"

02 — Assessment Auditor

Understanding what
the results mean.

Assessment Auditor is a free bilingual tool that helps test developers evaluate the quality of an existing talent assessment tool. It's mapped to professional assessment standards, covering psychometric integrity, equity and defensibility, and strategic and operational fit, while reflecting the realities of applied practice. The tool generates an automatic report with a colour-coded overall rating, category scores on a five-point scale, and a visual quality spectrum meter — a clear, standards-based read on how good a tool really is, for newcomers and seasoned test developers alike.
